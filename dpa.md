This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between the
customer using Resource Planner & Gantt for Jira ("Customer") and Product Radar,
operated by **ФОП Гринюк Ілля Олександрович** ("Provider"), when the Provider
processes personal data on the Customer's behalf through the App.

## 1. Roles and Instructions

The Customer is the controller and the Provider is the processor for personal
data submitted to or made available through the App, except where applicable
law assigns a different role. The Provider will process that data only on the
Customer's documented instructions, including the agreement, App configuration,
and authorized user actions, unless law requires otherwise.

The Customer is responsible for the lawfulness and accuracy of its instructions,
notices, permissions, and legal basis for processing.

## 2. Details of Processing

- **Subject matter:** Jira planning data, project configuration, and limited
  user identifiers needed for App functionality.
- **Duration:** The subscription period and the deletion or platform-retention
  period described below.
- **Nature and purpose:** Reading Jira data, displaying planner and Gantt views,
  storing schedules and settings, applying authorized Jira updates, providing
  support, and meeting security or privacy obligations.
- **Data subjects:** Jira users, assignees, project members, administrators, and
  individuals referenced in customer-provided Jira content.
- **Personal data:** Atlassian account identifiers, display names, avatar URLs,
  issue metadata, planning dates and durations, user-associated settings, and
  any personal data the Customer enters into Jira fields processed by the App.
- **Special-category data:** Not intentionally required. The Customer should not
  use the App to process special-category or highly sensitive data unless it has
  determined that doing so is lawful and appropriate.

## 3. Confidentiality

The Provider will ensure that persons authorized to process Customer personal
data are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations and receive access
only as needed to perform their responsibilities.

## 4. Security

The Provider will maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures
for risks within its control. The App uses Atlassian Forge hosting, encrypted
transport, hosted storage, authentication, Jira permission checks, dependency
review, and incident-response procedures. Details are available in the
[Cloud Security Statement](./security-statement.html).

## 5. Subprocessors

The Customer authorizes the subprocessors listed on the
[Subprocessors page](./subprocessors.html). Atlassian provides Jira Cloud and
Forge infrastructure for the App. The Provider will require subprocessors under
its control to protect personal data consistently with applicable obligations.

The Provider will publish material changes to the subprocessor list before a
new subprocessor begins processing App data where reasonably practicable. A
Customer with a reasonable data-protection objection may contact the Provider.

## 6. Data Subject Requests

Taking into account the nature of processing, the Provider will reasonably
assist the Customer with requests to access, correct, delete, restrict, object,
or port personal data where required by law and technically feasible. The
Provider may ask the Customer to use Jira or App administration controls first.

## 7. Security Incidents

The Provider will notify the Customer without undue delay after confirming a
personal-data breach affecting Customer data where notification is required by
law. Notices will include available information about the nature, likely
consequences, and mitigation. Customer remains responsible for notifications it
must make as controller.

## 8. Compliance Assistance

Taking into account the nature of processing and information available, the
Provider will reasonably assist with security reviews, breach obligations, data
protection impact assessments, and regulator consultations where required. The
Provider may provide current policies, architecture information, or platform
documentation instead of permitting access that could compromise another
customer or platform security.

## 9. International Transfers

Data may be processed in locations supported by Atlassian Cloud and Forge under
Atlassian's applicable safeguards. If the Provider independently transfers
Customer personal data across a restricted border, the Provider will use a
legally recognized transfer mechanism where required.

## 10. Return, Deletion, and Retention

During use, authorized administrators may delete certain planning data through
App controls and may configure automatic retention. On termination or a valid
instruction, the Provider will delete or return personal data within its control
unless retention is legally required.

After uninstall, Atlassian's Forge platform retains hosted storage in a
soft-deleted state for 28 days before permanent deletion under Atlassian's
current platform process. The Provider cannot shorten that platform-controlled
period.

## 11. Audit Information

On reasonable written request, the Provider will make information available to
demonstrate compliance with this DPA, subject to confidentiality, security,
legal, and Atlassian platform limitations. On-site audits are available only
where legally required and after less intrusive information has proved
insufficient.

## 12. Order of Precedence

If this DPA conflicts with the Terms concerning processing of Customer personal
data, this DPA controls for that conflict. All other terms remain in effect.
