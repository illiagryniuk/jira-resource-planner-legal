# Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

Last updated: June 24, 2026

This DPA forms part of the agreement between the customer using Resource Planner
("Customer") and Product Radar ("Provider").

## 1. Roles
For personal data processed by the App on behalf of the Customer:
- Customer acts as Data Controller.
- Provider acts as Data Processor.

## 2. Subject Matter and Duration
- Subject matter: Jira planning data, app configuration, and limited user
  identifiers needed for app functionality.
- Duration: For the period the App is installed and until applicable data is
  deleted, expired, or removed following uninstall or request.

## 3. Nature and Purpose of Processing
Processing is performed to provide Jira planning features, including issue
planning, assignee scheduling, day-off handling, Gantt and planner views, and
related app administration.

## 4. Categories of Data Subjects
Data subjects may include:
- Jira users
- Project members and assignees
- Jira administrators using the App

## 5. Categories of Personal Data
Depending on use, the App may process:
- Atlassian account IDs
- Display names
- Avatar URLs
- User-associated planning preferences and configuration
- Jira issue data that may contain personal data entered by Customer users

## 6. Sub-processors
Provider uses Atlassian Cloud and Atlassian Forge as hosting and infrastructure
sub-processors. No additional operational sub-processors are intentionally used
for this App's standard functionality at this time.

## 7. Security Measures
Provider relies on Atlassian Cloud and Forge controls, including:
- TLS for data in transit
- Atlassian-managed infrastructure protections
- Forge hosted storage controls
- Access controls enforced through Atlassian authentication and permissions

## 8. Data Subject Rights
Provider will reasonably assist Customer in responding to lawful requests for
access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of personal data to
the extent required by applicable law and technically feasible.

## 9. International Transfers
Data may be processed through Atlassian Cloud infrastructure in accordance with
Atlassian's applicable transfer mechanisms and safeguards.

## 10. Deletion and Retention
Data is retained only as needed for App functionality or compliance and may be
deleted through App controls, administrative action, uninstall, or verified
request. Certain planning records are also subject to configurable retention
settings.

## 11. Audit Information
Provider will make reasonable information available to demonstrate compliance
with this DPA, subject to confidentiality, security, and platform limitations.
