This Privacy Policy explains how Product Radar ("we," "us," or "our") handles
personal data when Resource Planner & Gantt for Jira (the "App") is installed
on and used with an Atlassian Jira Cloud site.

## 1. Provider and Scope

The App is provided under the Product Radar brand by **ФОП Гринюк Ілля
Олександрович**, an individual entrepreneur based in Netishyn, Khmelnytskyi
region, Ukraine.

This policy applies to data processed by the App. Atlassian independently
governs data processed through Jira Cloud, Atlassian accounts, Marketplace
billing, and the Forge platform under Atlassian's own terms and policies.

## 2. What the App Does

The App provides resource planning, issue scheduling, assignee timelines,
Gantt visualization, day-off management, and related project configuration.

## 3. Data the App Processes

Depending on the Jira data and features used, the App may process:

- Jira issue metadata, including issue keys, summaries, statuses, issue types,
  priorities, parent relationships, dates, estimates, assignees, and changelog
  data needed for planning.
- Atlassian user information supplied by Jira, including account identifiers,
  display names, and avatar URLs.
- Planning records and App settings, including planned dates and durations,
  assignee order and visibility, day offs, selected Gantt epics, expanded rows,
  retention settings, and working-day configuration.
- Limited technical and privacy-reporting metadata needed to operate the App,
  investigate errors, and support Atlassian personal-data reporting requests.

The App does not request passwords, payment card data, or Atlassian API tokens
from end users.

## 4. Sources of Data

Data comes from Jira Cloud APIs, user actions within the App, and configuration
generated while the App is used. Marketplace subscription and billing data is
handled by Atlassian.

## 5. Purposes and Legal Bases

We process data to provide the App, save schedules and settings, apply changes
requested by authorized users, provide support, maintain security, and meet
legal or platform obligations. Depending on the circumstances and applicable
law, this processing is based on performance of a contract, legitimate
interests in operating and securing the App, compliance with legal obligations,
or consent where required.

For personal data submitted to Jira by a customer, the customer generally
determines the purpose of processing and acts as controller. Product Radar acts
as processor to the extent described in the [DPA](./dpa.html).

## 6. Storage, Residency, and Egress

App planning data is stored in Atlassian Forge hosted storage. The App does not
use a separate Product Radar application database or external runtime backend,
and it is not designed to send Jira data to unrelated third parties during
normal operation. Atlassian provides the Jira Cloud and Forge infrastructure,
including applicable Forge data-residency capabilities.

See [Data Handling and Retention](./data-handling.html) and
[Subprocessors](./subprocessors.html) for more detail.

## 7. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is made available to Atlassian as necessary
for Jira Cloud, Forge hosting, storage, authentication, Marketplace licensing,
and platform compliance. We may disclose information when legally required, to
protect rights or security, or in connection with a lawful business transfer.

## 8. Retention and Deletion

Planning records remain in Forge hosted storage while needed to provide the App
unless they are deleted through App controls, an administrator action, a valid
request, or a configured project retention rule. Administrators can configure
automatic retention for certain planning data and can clear stored project
planning data.

After uninstall, Atlassian's Forge platform retains hosted storage in a
soft-deleted state for 28 days before permanent deletion under Atlassian's
current platform process. Atlassian controls this platform-level period.

## 9. Security

We use Forge authentication, Jira permission controls, hosted storage, and
encrypted transport provided by Atlassian. Additional information is available
in the [Cloud Security Statement](./security-statement.html).

## 10. International Transfers

Data may be processed in locations used by Atlassian Cloud and Forge. Atlassian
is responsible for the safeguards it provides for transfers performed through
its infrastructure. Product Radar will apply legally required safeguards to any
transfer for which it is responsible.

## 11. Privacy Rights and Requests

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights to access, correct,
delete, restrict, port, or object to processing of personal data, and to lodge a
complaint with a supervisory authority. Because a Jira customer controls most
Jira content, requests should normally be sent first to that customer's Jira
administrator.

To submit a request concerning data controlled by Product Radar, email
[illia.gryniuk@product-radar.com](mailto:illia.gryniuk@product-radar.com). We may need to
verify the requester and coordinate with the relevant Jira site administrator.

## 12. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for organizations using Jira Cloud and is not directed to
children. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children.

## 13. Documentation Site

These public pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical
request data when a visitor opens this documentation site under GitHub's own
privacy terms. The App does not send Jira data to this site.

## 14. Changes

We may update this policy as the App, law, or platform requirements change. The
date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be announced
through an appropriate product, listing, or support channel where required.
