# Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Product Radar ("we," "us," "our") handles
personal data when you use the Resource Planner app ("App") for Jira Cloud.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to the App and the data processed through it when installed
on an Atlassian Cloud site.

## 2. What the App Does
The App provides Jira planning features such as resource planning, issue
scheduling, assignee timelines, Gantt visualization, day-off management, and
related project planning configuration.

## 3. Data We Process
The App processes Jira data made available by Atlassian APIs, including:
- Issue metadata such as keys, summaries, status, issue type, priority, parent
  relationships, due dates, estimates, and planning dates.
- User-related identifiers and profile data supplied by Jira, such as accountId,
  displayName, and avatar URL.
- App configuration and planning records created by users, such as issue plan
  dates, assignee ordering and visibility, manually added Gantt epics, expanded
  sections, retention settings, and day-off entries.
- GDPR reporting metadata required to support Atlassian's personal data reporting
  obligations.

## 4. Personal Data We Store
The App stores personal data in Atlassian Forge hosted storage where needed for
app functionality. This can include Atlassian account IDs and related planning
preferences or configuration data associated with users.

We do not operate a separate external application database for this App.

## 5. Sources of Data
We receive data from:
- Atlassian Jira Cloud APIs
- User actions within the App
- App-generated configuration and audit metadata

## 6. Why We Process Data
We process data to:
- Display Jira issues in planner and Gantt views
- Save planning schedules and assignee-specific configuration
- Respect day-off and retention settings
- Support Jira issue updates initiated by authorized users
- Maintain app functionality, troubleshooting, and privacy compliance

## 7. Where Data Is Stored
App data is stored in Atlassian Forge hosted storage and processed within
Atlassian Cloud infrastructure.

## 8. Data Sharing
We do not sell personal data. We do not intentionally share App data with
third-party services outside Atlassian Cloud for this App's normal operation.

Atlassian acts as an infrastructure and platform provider for Forge-hosted
services and Jira Cloud.

## 9. Retention
Stored data is retained for as long as needed to provide the App or until it is
deleted through app controls, administrative action, uninstall, or a valid data
deletion request. The App also includes retention controls for certain planning
data.

## 10. Security
We rely on Atlassian Forge and Atlassian Cloud security controls, including
transport encryption and hosted platform protections. For more detail, see the
[Cloud Security Statement](./security-statement.html).

## 11. International Transfers
Data may be processed in locations used by Atlassian Cloud and Forge. Any
cross-border handling of data is subject to Atlassian's applicable safeguards.

## 12. Your Rights
Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or
object to certain processing of personal data. To make a request, contact us at
[productt.radarr@gmail.com](mailto:productt.radarr@gmail.com).

## 13. Children's Privacy
The App is not intended for use by children, and we do not knowingly collect
personal data directly from children.
