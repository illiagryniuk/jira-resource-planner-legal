## What to Include

To help us investigate, include:

- Jira Cloud site URL and project key.
- Affected issue keys, without confidential descriptions or attachments.
- Whether the problem occurs in Resource Planner, Gantt chart, or both.
- The action taken, expected result, and result shown.
- Browser name and approximate date and time of the incident.
- A screenshot with confidential information removed.
- App version, when visible in Atlassian administration.

Do not send passwords, API tokens, session cookies, recovery codes, financial
information, or other secrets.

## Common Checks

- Confirm the current user can view and update the affected Jira issue.
- Refresh only the affected assignee or epic before reloading the full page.
- Confirm the Marketplace subscription is active or in trial when editing is
  required.
- Confirm Jira original estimates and scheduling fields are available to the
  current user.
- Ask a Jira administrator to verify issue security, project permissions, and
  workflow transition permissions.

## Security Reports

Do not use a public issue or public discussion for a suspected vulnerability.
Email [productt.radarr@gmail.com](mailto:productt.radarr@gmail.com) with the
subject `Security report: Resource Planner & Gantt for Jira` and include:

- A clear description and affected feature.
- Reproduction steps or a proof of concept.
- Potential impact.
- Whether active exploitation or data exposure is suspected.
- A secure way to contact the reporter.

We target acknowledgement within two business days. Please allow reasonable
time for investigation and remediation before public disclosure.

## Privacy and Data Requests

For access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests, email
[productt.radarr@gmail.com](mailto:productt.radarr@gmail.com). Jira issue data is
generally controlled by the customer's Jira site administrator, so we may direct
the requester to that administrator or ask for verification.
